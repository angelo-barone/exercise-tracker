YOUR GYM LOGBOOK: HOW TO USE IT AND LOOK AFTER IT
(Written for you, Angelo. No code knowledge needed.)

OPEN IT
Tap the Logbook icon (dumbbell) on your home screen. Pick Minto, Montpetit or Other. Other is for any other gym: it shows every exercise. That is it.
It works with no wifi or signal.

FIRST TIME ON A PHONE
1. Open https://angelo-barone.github.io/exercise-tracker/ in Safari.
2. Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Leave "Open as Web App" on.
3. Open it from the new icon. Tap Plan, then Restore from a backup, pick your backup file.
4. Tap Back up now once.

EVERY DAY USE
- Pick the day, tap an exercise, enter weight and reps, tap an intensity number 1 to 10 (by feel), tap Log.
- Drop set: tap "+ drop set" under a set, enter the lighter weight and reps.
- Change an exercise: open it, tap "Edit name, gym, sets & reps". Arrows move it up or down. Delete asks twice.
- Add an exercise: tap the add button at the bottom of the day.
- Full Body: tap Full Body next to Legs. It is its own workout, not part of the week, and it never changes your rest day. It uses the same exercises as Push, Pull and Legs, so your history is shared. There you cannot delete, move or add; that list changes only through the program file.
- The top right shows "All saved". If it says "not saved", tap it to retry and tell me.

BACKUPS (this is the one thing that protects your data)
Your data lives on your phone only. If the phone is reset or you delete the app, it is gone unless you have a backup file.
- Plan tab, Your data, Back up now. Save the file to iCloud Drive or Files.
- Do it once a week, or after any big change. The app nags you after 14 days.
- New phone or lost data: Restore from a backup and pick the newest file.

CHANGING YOUR WORKOUT PROGRAM
Tell your Health and Wellness chat what you want changed. It edits the program file and tells you when it is published.
Then open the app with signal. A banner says a new program is ready. Tap it, read what changes, tap Apply update.
Nothing changes until you tap Apply. Your logged workouts are never touched. You can still edit single exercises in the app yourself, but the program file is the master list.
First time only: in the Health and Wellness chat, use Add folder and pick Dev\exercise-tracker so it can see program.json.

UPDATES
I update the app on GitHub. Close it fully and open it twice to get the newest version. Your data is never touched by an update.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- Do not delete the app. Deleting it wipes your data.
- Back up first if you can, then message me what you see.
- Any Claude Code session in the exercise-tracker folder reads CLAUDE.md and knows how the app works.
